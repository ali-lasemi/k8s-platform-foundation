# Roadmap

## v1.0

The v1.0 platform foundation includes:

- reproducible k3s bootstrap;
- Flux GitOps;
- ingress and TLS automation;
- DNS automation;
- policy-as-code security;
- encrypted GitOps Secrets;
- metrics and centralized logging;
- backup and disaster recovery;
- lifecycle automation;
- progressive delivery;
- multi-environment promotion;
- architecture documentation;
- production operational runbooks.

## Post-v1.0 candidates

Future work may include:

- Terraform infrastructure provisioning;
- distributed storage;
- multi-cluster GitOps;
- OIDC identity integration;
- workload identity;
- policy exception governance;
- SBOM generation;
- image signing and verification;
- supply-chain security;
- SLO and error-budget automation;
- chaos engineering;
- automated disaster recovery exercises.

Post-v1.0 work should remain focused on production platform engineering rather
than expanding into unrelated examples.
